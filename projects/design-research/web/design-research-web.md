## Working title
Design research for AI prototyping at Walmart  (working)

## One-line summary
I led research with 16 designers and others to learn where AI-assisted prototyping breaks, and presented it to stakeholders and reviewers to shape the direction for a governed prototyping tool.

## Context
Designers were already using AI across their work. The question was not whether they would, but where it let them down and what a Walmart tool would have to do differently.

## Method (confirmed numbers)
- 16 participants: 8 in India (IDC) and 8 in the US.
- 13 interviews and a survey with 37 responses.
- April to June 2026.

## Findings (counts are participants out of 16)
1. The toolchain is fragmented: 16 of 16.
2. Living Design compliance is checked by hand, and late: 15 of 16.
3. AI output is not trusted enough to hand off: 14 of 16.
4. Handoff is where design intent is most vulnerable: 14 of 16.

## Direction that emerged
- Two needs: PRD/prompt to early concepts, and final design to a governed, review-ready handoff.
- The opportunity is not faster prototypes but governed ones: real Living Design components, controlled refinement, traceable handoff.
