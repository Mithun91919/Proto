Localization for TOTAL OS
TOTAL is a mobile OS built by Hike with services that work Without the Internet and are supported for 8 local languages
Role: Product Research

Disciplines: Research | Agency Interaction

Year: 2017
Challenges & Goals

1. Translations from English to 8 Indian scripts.

 

2. Interaction with the Language Service Provider and establishing a localization framework.

 

3. Proof reading by internal language experts.

 

4. Code implementation.

Research

To get better results from the agency a proper framework was tried and established, With more focus on maintaining the intent in all the 8 Languages across 4 Projects. You can find the Process Doc here.
